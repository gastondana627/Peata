The Rescue & Reunite Hub

The Hub is a bundled toolkit with five distinct features designed to empower pet owners. Click on any tool to learn more about its specific function and the technology behind it.

📢

Lost Pet Reporting

Instantly create and share a digital "lost pet" poster.

How it Works

Users fill out an `st.form` with pet details and upload a photo. This data is saved to our database and generates a shareable alert for social media.

🗺️

Shelter Tracker

Find nearby shelters and rescue organizations quickly.

How it Works

Queries our database for local shelters based on user location. Results are displayed in a table and can be plotted on an interactive map using `st.map`.

🔍

AI-Powered Search

Use a photo to search for a found pet in our database.

How it Works

A photo of a found pet is sent to Vertex AI's vision model, which extracts features (e.g., "golden retriever," "blue collar") to find matches in the lost pets database.

🤝

Community Alerts

Notify the local Peata community about a lost or found pet.

How it Works

Triggers a notification (future feature) to other users in the geographical area, creating a network of helpers to amplify the search effort.

📚

Resource Guide

Access helpful articles and guides for pet owners.

How it Works

A curated collection of static content on pet safety, what to do when a pet is lost, and local regulations, organized using `st.expander` for a clean UI.

Designing for Emotion & Inclusion

Our goal is to bridge the gap between technology and non-technical users. We achieve this through an empathetic design philosophy that prioritizes emotional connection, accessibility, and compelling storytelling, ensuring Peata is a tool for everyone in the community.

UI/UX & Emotional Design

  • 🎨

    Custom UI & Personality

    Using custom PNG/MP4 buttons with our Peata mascot to create a warm, personable helper, not a cold app.

  • ❤️

    Empathetic Copy

    Crafting UI text with a warm, helpful, and reassuring tone to support users during stressful times.

  • 🎬

    Storytelling Focus

    Our video demo will use real-life scenarios of families being reunited with their pets to create a powerful emotional connection.

Core Accessibility Principles

  • 👁️

    High Contrast & Alt Text

    Ensuring text is readable for visually impaired users and that all images have descriptive alt text for screen readers.

  • ⌨️

    Keyboard Navigation

    Making sure every feature in the app is fully usable without a mouse to support users with mobility impairments.

  • 字幕

    Subtitles & Transcription

    Providing subtitles for all video content to assist hearing-impaired users and improve comprehension for all.
